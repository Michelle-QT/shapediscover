ShapeDiscover: cover-learning benchmark results

Homology recovery and the over-optimization study. Generated 2026-06-21 from committed result CSVs.

ShapeDiscover learns a fuzzy cover whose nerve should recover the topology of a point cloud. The headline metric is the homology-recovery quotient (fraction of the nerve filtration with the exactly-correct Betti vector). Over-optimization: at fixed data and n_cover, with early stopping off, recovery rises to an interior-iteration peak then declines toward convergence. All tables are computed directly from the CSVs in benchmarks/results/.

5. Future directions

  • Anti-element-death / loss-balance lever (best-grounded candidate): a per-element measure floor or a higher default measure weight. Evidence: measure=4 removes the donut's over-optimization. Untested: generalization to Klein bottle / RP² / raw anisotropic torus, cost on the simple manifolds, sampling-invariance. No change to the default algorithm has been made.
  • Whitening as preprocessing for linearly anisotropic data: validated on the flat torus (removes the anisotropy-induced over-optimization); does not fix the donut's nonlinear/local anisotropy.
  • High intrinsic-dimensional homology: gated by the dense-nerve blow-up; needs genuinely sparse covers before n_cover can be pushed higher.
  • Persistence-based clustering: the current connected-components route under-segments real high-dimensional data to a single cluster.
